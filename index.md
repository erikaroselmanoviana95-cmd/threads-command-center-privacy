# Privacy Policy — Threads Command Center

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Threads Command Center ("the App") is a locally operated tool designed to help its operator manage connected Threads accounts, discover public Threads posts using keywords, organize potential business engagement opportunities, and prepare reply drafts.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and protected when using the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the App may process:

- Threads account ID and username.
- Threads access tokens required to connect an account.
- Public Threads post IDs, text, usernames, timestamps, and permalinks returned by the Threads API.
- Search keywords and campaign configurations.
- AI-generated reply drafts and approval status.
- Basic application logs and operational information.

The App does not intentionally collect passwords for Threads accounts.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively to:

- Authenticate and connect authorized Threads accounts.
- Search for public Threads posts using configured keywords.
- Organize and review relevant posts.
- Prepare suggested reply drafts.
- Allow the operator to review and approve replies before publication.
- Maintain application security and troubleshoot technical issues.

The App does not automatically publish replies. Human approval is required before a reply can be published.

## 4. Meta Platform Data

The App accesses Threads Platform Data through the official Meta Threads API and only uses data for the purposes described in this policy.

We do not sell, rent, or commercially distribute Threads Platform Data.

We do not use Threads Platform Data to build advertising profiles or unrelated user profiles.

## 5. AI Processing

If AI-assisted features are enabled, selected post text and relevant campaign context may be sent to the AI service configured by the operator to generate suggested replies or classify posts.

AI-generated content is treated as a suggestion and must be reviewed by a human before publication.

The App does not intentionally send Threads access tokens or account credentials to the AI service.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

The App is operated locally by its operator.

Application data is stored in a local SQLite database. Threads access tokens are encrypted before being stored using Fernet encryption.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the App's operational purposes.

The operator may remove connected account information, discovered post records, and reply drafts when they are no longer needed.

Users may request disconnection of their Threads account or deletion of associated information by contacting the email address below.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following services:

- **Meta Threads API:** Account authentication and Threads platform functionality.
- **AI service provider:** AI-assisted processing when enabled by the operator.
- **GitHub Pages:** Hosting this publicly accessible Privacy Policy.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 9. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Information is only shared with third-party services when necessary to provide the functionality described in this policy, or when required by applicable law.
